About Creature Park

A learning adventure game for kids: answer questions, earn coins, and collect one-of-a-kind creatures in your own park.

Questions span animals, space, the human body, history, and more, and they grow with the player. Coins earned answering questions go toward creatures no one else has, each one generated uniquely for your park.

Features

  • Hundreds of questions across school topics
  • Earn coins and collect one-of-a-kind creatures
  • Gentle pace made for kids
  • Plays in the browser on any device
