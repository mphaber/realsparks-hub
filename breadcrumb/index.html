About Breadcrumb

A calm daily link game: start from one article, follow the breadcrumb trail of links to reach the target in as few hops as you can. No timer, ever.

Start at one Wikipedia article and follow its links to reach the target in as few hops as you can. Play the daily wander, or work through themed trails of levels at your own pace; Casual play hides par and stars entirely, and every wander saves itself.

Features

  • A new daily wander every day, gentlest on Mondays and deepest on Sundays
  • Themed level trails with waypoint and banned-word challenges
  • No timer, unlimited undo, and auto-save
  • Share your result without spoiling the route
