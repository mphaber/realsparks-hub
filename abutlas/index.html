About Abutlas

Abutlas is a geography puzzle where neighboring states and countries are shuffled like puzzle pieces and you rebuild the map. Pieces start unmarked, so the clues are the real outlines, rivers, and coastlines; drag pieces together and neighbors snap when they line up in the right arrangement.

Work through 50 levels across 10 regions, from New England to East and Southeast Asia, or take the two fresh dailies that arrive every day: one US, one from anywhere in the world. No accounts, no ads, and your progress saves on your device.

Features

  • 50 levels across 10 regions, from New England to East and Southeast Asia
  • Two fresh dailies every day, one US and one Anywhere, growing through the week
  • Three modes: Casual untimed, Pro against the clock, and Diabolical with rotation
  • Hints in the form of a name, a capital, a landmark, or the region's outline
  • A lifetime Cartographer rank, from Armchair Traveler to Grand Cartographer
  • Pinch to zoom the board, with real rivers drawn on the pieces as clues
  • No accounts, no ads; plays in the browser and saves on your device
