About Scribble Siege

A hand-drawn paper flick game: flick fighters across the page in Space, Knights, and Tanks battles, and destroy the enemy base in 3 hits.

Flick your fighters across the notebook page, bounce them off the scenery, and knock out the enemy base in three hits. Every battlefield is drawn by hand, and the soundtrack is a real lunchroom.

Features

  • Three battlefields: Space, Knights, and Tanks
  • Flick physics with ricochets
  • Destroy the enemy base in 3 hits
  • Hand-drawn art and real lunchroom sound
