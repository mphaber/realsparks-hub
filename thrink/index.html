About Thrink

A quick, brain-teasing tile puzzle for grown-ups: place tiles, link matching groups of three, and chase chain reactions.

Tiles of 64 and up merge in a connected pair; smaller tiles need three or more. A daily puzzle gives everyone the same board each day, free play is endless, and best scores are kept for each mode.

Features

  • A new daily puzzle every day
  • Free play mode with best scores saved
  • Bag shuffle for when the board turns against you
  • Optional music, off by default
  • No timers, no lives, no ads, no undo
