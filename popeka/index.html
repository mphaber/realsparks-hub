About Popeka!

A relaxing bubble-pop game with 500 levels: tap matching bubbles, clear the board, and unlock new worlds.

Tap a group of two or more matching bubbles to pop them and clear the board to finish the level; stranded single bubbles pop themselves. Undo is unlimited, restarting is free, and your progress saves itself as you go.

Features

  • 500 levels across themed worlds
  • Every board is guaranteed clearable, with a par to beat on every level
  • Unlimited undo and free restarts
  • New bubble types arrive as you travel, including Zap and Twin bubbles
  • No timers, no energy, no ads
  • Also free on itch.io
