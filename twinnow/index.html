About Twinnow

The word game where duplicates die. Build words from a source word's letters against the clock: any word another player also found cancels, only unique words score, and long words pay extra. Play with 2 to 4 players passing one device, or go solo against the computer.

One long source word goes up, the clock starts, and everyone lists every word they can build from its letters. When time runs out, the lists are revealed side by side: any word on two lists is crossed out, and the words only you found score, with longer words worth steeply more. Words are checked against a Scrabble-style dictionary and letter counts are enforced as you type, so there is nothing to argue about at the reveal.

Features

  • Pass and play for 2 to 4 players on one device, or solo against the computer
  • Three computer difficulties that play like a person, not a dictionary
  • Strict letter counts by default, with a Loose mode if your house rules differ
  • Scrabble-style dictionary, with a Generous option for modern words
  • One quick round, or a 3 or 5 round match
  • Your match saves itself, so you can pick it up again
  • Also free on itch.io
