About Xenofront

A turn-based strategy game of hex warfare: command miniature armies, capture territory, and outsmart the enemy across alien worlds.

Every hex matters: what your units stand on changes how hard they are to hit, and holding ground is how campaigns are won. Free in the browser, with nothing to download.

Features

  • Turn-based tactics on hex maps
  • Capture and hold territory
  • A campaign across alien worlds
  • Free in the browser, no downloads
