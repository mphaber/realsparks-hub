The Skies

Every sky you finish stays lit.

How to play

The stars have drifted out of their pictures and scattered across the night pasture. Your ripples bring them home.

Tap the sky to send out a ripple. A ripple nudges every star away from your tap, so tap below a star to lift it, or beside one to steer it sideways.

Guide each star into a glowing ring and it settles. Fill every ring and the picture takes shape.

No timer, no score, and no way to lose a star. Undo and Restart are always there, and however many ripples it takes is the right number.

The skies ahead hold gentle surprises: moonrocks whose gravity bends a passing star, currents that carry you, a comet that never rests, heavy stars, tethered pairs, and rings that wander.

Further on, twin gates whisk a star across the sky, beacons light dim stars so they can settle, and still nebulas let a star glide softly to a stop. Each new sky shows you its trick when you arrive.
