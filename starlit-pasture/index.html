About Starlit Pasture

The stars have drifted out of their pictures and scattered across the night pasture. Tap to send a ripple through the sky, nudge each star home into its glowing ring, and watch the picture take shape.

Features

  • 100 skies in 10 sets
  • Gravity wells, drifting currents, comet stars, twin gates, and still nebulas
  • No timer and no way to fail: play at the pace of a quiet evening
  • Soft tonal chimes that ring as each star comes home
